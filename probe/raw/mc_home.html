The nineteenth edition of the European Championship was held at the Westfalenhalle in Dortmund from October 22nd to 25th. The 32 best players of the European Tour season competed for the title and a share of the £750,000 prize fund. It was Ross Smith who ultimately claimed the title for the second time, after defeating German favourite Martin Schindler 11–8 in the final.
